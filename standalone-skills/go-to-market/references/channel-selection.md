# Channel Selection

Use this guide for channel comparisons. The fit examples are hypotheses to investigate, not proven audience matches.

## Compare Against The Project

| Dimension | Evidence or question |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | Where do intended users discuss or solve this task, in this market and language? |
| Intent | Are they seeking advice, evaluating a tool, buying or returning? |
| Access | Which exact account, Page, community or consented list can the owner use? |
| Content | Can the team make credible material in useful formats? |
| Capacity | Who produces assets, replies and moderates? |
| Conversion | Which available destination and meaningful user action follow the content? |
| Measurement | Which aggregate source, baseline and window can actually be read? |
| Uncertainty | What could change the recommendation or rule the channel out? |

Customer conversations, relevant account activity and attributed product actions are stronger fit evidence than platform popularity.
No evidence means a provisional experiment, not an inferred audience.

## Channel Hypotheses

| Candidate | Fit worth testing | Content direction | Reasons to defer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X (Twitter) | Intended users discuss the task in reachable accounts or conversations, in the project's market and language | Specific lessons, real demos, useful replies and truthful release updates | Weak audience evidence, unavailable account access or no reply/moderation capacity |
| Threads | A reachable audience discusses the problem and the team can join conversations | Specific observations, tips, small demos and honest questions | No audience evidence or no reply owner |
| Facebook Pages | A relevant Page audience or a credible acquisition path exists | Useful explanations, visual proof, updates and clear next steps | Empty Page without an acquisition path, missing access or weak audience fit |
| LinkedIn | The task belongs to an identifiable professional role or workflow | Work problems, evidence, lessons and demonstrations | Professional context adds little or claims lack proof |
| Instagram / short video | The benefit is visible and the team can produce useful demonstrations | Show the task and result in the selected format | Production exceeds capacity or the benefit cannot be shown truthfully |
| Relevant communities | A named community discusses the problem and permits participation | Answer real questions, disclose affiliation and follow local rules | Promotion prohibited, permission unclear or drive-by advertising |
| Product Hunt | A usable product fits early adopters and has a launch reply owner | Demonstration, plain description and maker context | Unusable release, weak audience overlap or no reply capacity |
| Owned email | A relevant consented list and useful reason to contact it exist | Useful updates, onboarding and focused announcements | No permissioned list, unclear purpose or missing unsubscribe handling |
| Search / articles | Evidenced information needs and an appropriate public destination exist | Original material answering real tasks | Private-only product, no discovery surface or no capacity for a longer feedback window |

A Facebook Page is not a Group. Check Group rules and identity before proposing Group distribution.
Founder-led and brand posts need separate account and voice decisions.
Paid media, partner outreach and direct messages require a separately scoped plan and authorization.

For X, distinguish the founder account, brand account and participation in another person's conversation.
Use relevant public posts as audience or content examples, with author, date, original URL and observed context.
Label search snippets, mirrored text and truncated embeds; they do not establish full-thread or media inspection.
Popularity and another maker's launch results do not prove demand for this product.
Choose a bounded experiment with an available destination and response owner; use the existing experiment record.

## Primary Sources And Refresh

Observed 2026-10-15. Refresh material facts before use; these sources do not establish project-specific market fit.

- [Meta's Threads guidance](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/find-your-community-with-new-threads-educational-insights/) emphasizes original content and conversations.
  Its reported correlations do not establish a project's best cadence or sales outcome.
- [LinkedIn's distribution guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a516930/) describes professional relevance.
  Validate the actual role and network rather than assuming every B2B product fits.
- [Reddit's rules](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043503951-What-are-Reddit-s-rules) preserve community-specific rules.
  [Its spam guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/28012014962580-How-do-I-keep-spam-out-of-my-community) describes differing practices.
  One community's promotional ratio is not a platform-wide entitlement.
- [Product Hunt's launch guide](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17350772-launch-guide) covers preparation and maker participation, and prohibits direct upvote requests.
  A listing, vote count or launch-day visit is not retained usage.
- [Google's campaign URL guidance](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en) explains referral parameters.
  A tagged link does not prove analytics or conversion measurement works.

[Facebook Business help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/942827662903020) and [Threads developer docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/threads/) were access-limited during research.
Do not cite unseen contents as verified instructions.
Before execution, inspect current official guidance and the intended account through an available read-only route.
Format limits, scheduling support, API permissions and regional availability remain unverified until checked.
An account or API access gap does not prevent strategy and draft preparation.

X sources checked on 2026-10-18: [post types](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/types-of-posts),
[threads](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/create-a-thread) and [metrics](https://docs.x.com/x-api/fundamentals/metrics).
These document formats and measurement access, not audience fit or guaranteed distribution.
Refresh the selected account's app/API capabilities before execution; do not assume subscription features or analytics access.
