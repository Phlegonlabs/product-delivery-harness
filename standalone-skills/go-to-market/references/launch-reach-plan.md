# Launch Reach Plan

Use this template when the project has a PRD `## Growth Contract` or another owner-approved launch window.
The plan turns the launch window into a reviewable distribution list. Every item stays a proposal.
No item publishes, schedules, buys placement, submits to a store or contacts a journalist.

## Derive From The Growth Contract

Read the contract's Launch Window table first and keep its rows. Extend them; do not replace them.
The north star, re-engagement mechanism and discovery surfaces decide which classes matter for this launch.
Give every item one existing destination. Send a missing destination to its product or implementation owner.
Name a readback metric that exists, or record the activation gap that would verify it.

Class meanings:

- `owned`: accounts, Pages, consented lists and product surfaces the team controls.
- `earned`: communities, directories, press and other places that decide independently.
- `search`: public pages, structured data and listing text that reach search and answer engines.
- `store`: store-listing assets, screenshots and release notes for the app or extension.

## Sequencing

- Prepare search and store assets before Day 0; indexing and store review need lead time.
- Place earned items on Day 0 to Day 2, after the destination works.
- Repeat owned items across the window at the team's real reply capacity.
- Hold the Day 7 and Day 30 readbacks against the contract's north-star targets.

## Readback

Record each metric's aggregate source with the item, or name the gap that keeps it unmeasured.
Return the Day 7 and Day 30 results to the owner with the launch record.
Weak results change the next plan. They do not justify ad spend, ranking promises or link schemes.
