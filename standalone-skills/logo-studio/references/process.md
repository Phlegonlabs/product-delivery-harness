# The Round Process

One round runs from the audit to the presented shortlist. Give each round an ID, such as `LOGO-R2`.
The numbers below are defaults. The owner may change them; record the change in the brief.

## Audit

Collect the marks the audience sees beside this brand: direct competitors, the leaders of the field, and local neighbours.
Collect about twelve to twenty marks. Include every mark that shares the brand's colour.
Look at each mark at 128, 32 and 16 pixels.
Write what holds at 16 pixels and what fails.
Write two or three lines on what the field looks like and where the open space is.
Record each source link and the retrieval date. Keep the images outside the repository.

## Sketch Sheet

Draw at least 36 rough marks for a `new` scope, and at least 12 for a `refresh` scope.
Draw each sketch as real vector art, black on white, at one common size. Do not polish.
Number the sketches `S01` upward and give each a note of about five words.

Spread the sketches over four families, with at least six in each for a `new` scope and at least three in each for a `refresh` scope:

| Family | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| A. Wordmark | The word alone, with one custom feature |
| B. Letter symbol | A symbol built from the name's letters, beyond a plain initial: letter pairs, shared strokes, counters, negative space |
| C. Idea symbol | An abstract or pictorial symbol from what the brand is for |
| D. Free | Anything that fits the brief and none of the above |

At most one sketch in nine may be a plain initial.
Take the ideas for family C from the brief, not from the current interface design.
For a name in another script, apply the same families to that script.

## Cull Table

Score every sketch 0, 1 or 2 on each of the five tests:

| Test | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Appropriate | Does it feel right for the field and the brand's character? |
| Describable | Can one sentence without "and" describe it? |
| Memorable | Could a person draw it after two looks? |
| Small | Does it hold at 16 pixels in one colour? |
| Clear | Is it far from every mark in the audit? |

Drop a sketch that scores 0 on any test.
Write one line on each dropped sketch that looked promising, and why it failed.
Do not raise a score to keep a favourite.

## Shortlist

Select three to five sketches for refinement; five is the default.
Take them from at least three families. At most one may be a plain initial.
When fewer than three sketches pass the cull, sketch again before refining.

## Refined File Set

Produce these files for each concept:

- symbol
- wordmark
- horizontal lockup and stacked lockup
- one-colour black version and one-colour white version
- brand-colour version
- app icon artwork at 1024 pixels, full bleed
- favicon masters at 16 and 32 pixels, redrawn on the pixel grid

Every file is plain SVG paths with a `viewBox`.
Use no live text, no transforms, no gradients, no filters and no embedded raster image.
Name the files by round and concept, such as `r2-c1-symbol.svg`.

Write the construction of each concept in two or three lines.
Name the unit, the stroke, the key ratios and each correction by eye.
State the clear space as a multiple of a part of the mark, such as the wordmark's cap height.
State the smallest tested size of the lockup.

## Rank

Rank the concepts and give the reason for each position in one or two sentences.
List what still falls short for each concept, and what you did not verify.
Do not oversell. When no concept meets the three criteria, say so and propose the next round.
