# Stress Tests

Run every test on all concepts side by side, with the current mark as the before.
Use the places from the brief. A test in a place the brand never uses proves nothing.

## Small Sizes

- Show the symbol at 16, 24, 32, 60 and 180 pixels, black on white and white on black.
- Show the lockup at its smallest size, with a cap height of about 8 pixels.
- Below 24 pixels, test the redrawn favicon masters, not the scaled symbol.
- Fail a concept whose strokes fuse, whose counters close, or whose idea disappears.

## One Colour And Reversed

- Show each concept in black only, in white on the darkest ground, and in the brand colour.
- Measure the contrast of each colour pair. Record each pair below 3:1 and forbid it for small or thin shapes.
- Fail a concept that only its colour makes recognizable.

## In Context

Place each concept in the real places of the product. Reuse the approved HiFi or the live components when they exist.

- the narrow header and its open menu
- the wide header
- the footer
- the browser tab, on a light and a dark browser theme
- the app's first screen, in light and dark
- any other place the brief names: a store listing, an avatar, a receipt, print

Check that the symbol and the wordmark do not repeat the same letter side by side.
Check that the mark does not compete with the page's main element.

## App Icon

- Show the icon at 60 points on a light and a dark home screen, between two real-looking neighbours.
- Show the platform's dark and monochrome or tinted appearances when the target platform has them.
- Keep the symbol inside the platform's safe zone.
- The mask in a mock is an approximation. Record that the platform tool or a device was not used when that is the case.

## Three-Second Test

Look at the mark for three seconds, then write what a stranger would call it.
Write the honest answer, such as "a smile" or "a return arrow", and not the intended one.
When a person outside the work is available, ask that person and record the answer.
A second reading is acceptable when it does no harm. Record a harmful or a comic reading as a failure.

## Resemblance Check

Compare each concept with the audit set at 60 and 16 pixels, side by side.
Also compare it with well-known marks outside the field that share its shape or colour.
Change or drop a concept that a person could confuse with an existing mark.
Record the nearest mark for each concept and why the two differ.
This check is by eye. It is not a trademark search.

## Evidence

Render the tests in a real browser and save the captures beside the files.
Save one sheet with all concepts, one board per concept, and one sheet of the small sizes.
Read every capture before you report. A test that you did not look at is not a test.
Record the renderer, its version and the device scale. Name each test that you could not run.
Start no server for a capture when a local file renders the same page.
